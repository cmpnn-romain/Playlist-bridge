## Lineage

The repository layout, the versioning policy and the build and release setup come from
[Unplug](https://github.com/cmpnn-romain/Unplug), also GPLv3, which took them from the [Cairn launcher](https://github.com/Cairn-client/Launcher).
Playlist-bridge talks to the services through [ytmusicapi](https://github.com/sigma67/ytmusicapi) (MIT) and
[tidalapi](https://github.com/tamland/python-tidal) (LGPLv3).
